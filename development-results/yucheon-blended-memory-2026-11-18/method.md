# Confidence and similarity for memory admission

Source commit `d554ba5c57260363816a1701050d135d95b9b821` on `yucheon-2026-11-18`. The change applies to the unchosen preview in the two-candidate Ours protocol. With cosine similarity s and p = (s − 0.2) / 0.65, the allowed gap is `0.10 + 0.05 * max(0, 1 - abs(2*p - 1))`, with a peak of 0.15 at s = 0.525. Eligibility, finite scores and the cosine band [0.2, 0.85) still apply. The gap uses uniform mean continuation log probabilities. The selection score still uses 25% first-half and 75% second-half confidence and commits the selected rollout verbatim. The single added config field is `memory_quality_gate.similarity_gap_bonus`. The maximum bonus follows the proposed 0.15 example and the center follows from the existing band.
